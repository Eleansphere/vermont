# Sound credits

Every sound and piece of music in this folder is in the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Crediting is not required; we do it
anyway.

All files were cut, loudness-normalized and encoded to Ogg Vorbis with ffmpeg; effects are mono.
Apart from that, and the changes named below, they are unchanged.
