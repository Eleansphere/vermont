From **KayKit : Medieval Hexagon Pack 1.0** by Kay Lousberg (www.kaylousberg.com),

`hex_grass`, `hex_water`, `tree_single_A`, `tree_single_B`, `rock_single_A`, `hill_single_A`,
`mountain_A_grass`, `waterplant_A`, `waterplant_B`, `waterlily_A`, `tent`, `barrel`,
